Ask Siri about the air, check the forecast in its own tab, and page through your favourites right on the widget.

SIRI, SHORTCUTS AND CONTROL CENTER
Say "What's the air in Taiwan Air" for your nearest station, or "Taiwan Air air at Songshan" for a favourite: Siri tells you the AQI, its level, the main pollutant and what to do. "Get Air Quality" and "Open Station" are ready in Shortcuts and Spotlight and can go on the Action button. On iOS 18, add a Control Center control that shows the AQI and opens the station. Free for everyone.

A NEW FORECAST TAB
Air quality forecasts for every area of Taiwan now have their own tab, second from the left. Your area comes first, then your favourites' areas, with every day the forecast covers. The forecast text is now in English too.

WIDGETS AND APPLE WATCH
On medium and large widgets (Pro), tap ‹ or › to step through your favourites. Tap the index name on a Home Screen widget to switch between AQI, PM2.5, PM10, O3, CO, SO2 and NO2. In StandBy and Smart Stacks the widget comes to the top by itself when the air turns bad, and it now follows the language you choose in the app. On Apple Watch, the face complication is free; the number and more styles are part of Pro.

ALERTS, SHARING AND MORE
Favouriting a station now switches its alerts on, for up to 3 stations, and the alert settings sit near the top of every station page. Alerts are sent through a new delivery service. Share cards come in three shapes (post 4:5, story 9:16, chat 1:1), with a preview first and Save to Photos. Plus a welcome tour that opens once and can be replayed from Help, and faster map updates when you move through the timeline.
