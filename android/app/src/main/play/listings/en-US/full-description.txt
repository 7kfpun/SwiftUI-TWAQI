Taiwan Air shows you the air you are actually breathing, right now, measured by the government monitoring stations across Taiwan.

IS IT GETTING BETTER OR WORSE?
Every station page answers the question you actually have. A 24-hour AQI chart, coloured hour by hour, with a one-line verdict above it: improving, getting worse, or steady. The day's low, high and average sit underneath. No chart-reading required.

A LIVE MAP OF EVERY STATION

• Switch the whole map between AQI, PM2.5, PM10, O3, CO, SO2 and NO2
• Wind mode turns every marker into an arrow: which way the air is moving, and how hard
• Play back the past 24 hours on the map with the timeline
• A card shows the station nearest the middle of the map, with its reading and the time it was measured
• The map reopens where you left it

WHY IT MATTERS
Air pollution is not only a respiratory problem. It raises the risk of cardiovascular disease, stroke and other chronic illness, and in 2013 the International Agency for Research on Cancer classified outdoor air pollution as a Group 1 carcinogen. Children and older adults are affected most. Thirty seconds before you leave the house tells you whether to take the mask, or to run the purifier instead of opening the window.

WHERE THE DATA COMES FROM
Air quality readings, forecasts and station cameras are open data from Taiwan's Ministry of Environment (https://www.moenv.gov.tw). Weather is open data from the Central Weather Administration (https://www.cwa.gov.tw). We display it; we do not adjust it.
