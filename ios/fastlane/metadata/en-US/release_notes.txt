A redesigned station screen that tells you, in one line, whether the air is getting better or worse.

24-HOUR AIR QUALITY TREND
Open any station and see its last 24 hours of AQI, colour-coded hour by hour, with a plain-language verdict above the chart: improving, getting worse, or holding steady. The day's low, high and average sit underneath. The verdict compares averages rather than single readings, so one bad hour doesn't flip the answer.

A NEW STATION SCREEN
Station name, township, county and air quality zone now sit together in one header. All six pollutants are listed at once — no more switching between them one at a time. Wind, address, coordinates and health advice each have their own place, and every reading shows how many hours old it is.

BETTER SEARCH
Stations are grouped by air quality zone and ordered roughly north to south, and you can now search by township or county name instead of only by station name.

FASTER AND MORE STABLE
Rebuilt with the latest tooling, with updated maps and components throughout, plus a range of stability and performance fixes.
