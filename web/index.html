Taiwan Air 台灣空氣品質

Live air quality from every Ministry of Environment (環境部) monitoring station in Taiwan, on iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and Apple Vision Pro. 中文說明

Download on the App Store

What it does

  • Map of stations — AQI, PM2.5, PM10, O₃ and more for every station, colour-coded, with your nearest station picked for you if you allow location.
  • Station details — recent readings and trends, plus the station webcam (where available) with the last 24 hours of hourly frames.
  • Forecast — the official air quality zone forecast (空品區預報) for the days ahead.
  • Favourites — keep the stations you care about in one list.
  • Alerts — get a notification when a station crosses the level you choose, with quiet hours.
  • Widgets & Watch — Home Screen widgets, an Apple Watch app and complications.
  • Works offline — the last readings stay available without a connection.

Free with banner ads; a one-time in-app purchase removes them. No account needed.

台灣空氣品質

即時呈現環境部全台空氣品質監測站的觀測數值，支援 iPhone、iPad、Apple Watch 與 Apple Vision Pro。

  • 測站地圖：AQI、PM2.5、PM10、臭氧等指標一目了然；開啟定位即可自動選擇最近的測站。
  • 測站詳情：近期數值與趨勢，有攝影機的測站還可瀏覽即時影像及過去 24 小時的每小時畫面。
  • 空品區預報：官方發布的未來數日空氣品質預報。
  • 收藏：把常看的測站整理在同一個清單。
  • 警示通知：測站數值超過您設定的門檻時通知您，並可設定勿擾時段。
  • 小工具與 Apple Watch：主畫面小工具、Apple Watch App 與錶面複雜功能。
  • 離線瀏覽：沒有網路時仍可查看最近一次的數值。

免費下載，內含橫幅廣告，可透過一次性購買移除廣告。不需要註冊帳號。
