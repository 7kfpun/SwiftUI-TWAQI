Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy Last updated: October 31, 2026 Taiwan Air ("the App", App Store ID 1286516443, bundle com.kfpun.twaqi) shows air quality readings from government monitoring stations in Taiwan. It is made by an independent developer. We respect your privacy and keep data collection to a minimum. This policy explains what is collected, why, and who it is shared with. Where the air quality data comes from • Readings are open data published by Taiwan's Ministry of Environment (環境部, formerly the Environmental Protection Administration, Executive Yuan). The App fetches them through our own API hosted on Amazon Web Services. • That data describes monitoring stations, not people. Requests to our API carry only a country code, a station ID and a row limit — no account, no device identifier and no coordinates. Information stored on your device • The App has no accounts. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number or contacts. • Your settings stay on your device: the index you have selected (AQI, PM2.5, and so on), your favourite stations, the last map position, quiet-hours times, the nearest station last resolved, and whether you have bought the ad-free upgrade. • This on-device data is removed when you delete the App. Location • Location access is optional and is requested only while you are using the App ("Use location to select your nearest air quality monitoring station"). • Your coordinates are used on the device to centre the map and to pick the closest monitoring station. They are not sent to our API and we do not store them on our servers. • The map itself is drawn by the Google Maps SDK. Google may process location and device data for map display under Google's own privacy policy. • If you decline location access the App still works; it falls back to a default station and to whichever station you choose on the map. Notifications • Push notifications are delivered through Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service, which receive your device's push tokens. • If you turn on alerts for a station, we store on our own server a random identifier created for this device, its push tokens, the station, your chosen thresholds, your quiet-hours window and time zone, and the App's version and language, so the alert can be sent. This is not linked to your name or contacts. • Turning an alert off marks it inactive and it is no longer used; deleting the App stops delivery. Analytics and crash reporting • Google Firebase Analytics records anonymous usage events — for example which air index you select, opening a station, or starting and completing a purchase — together with standard device and app information. • Google Firebase Crashlytics, Bugsnag and Sentry receive crash and error reports: stack traces, device model, OS version and app version. These help us fix bugs. • None of this is used to identify you personally, and we do not link it to your name or contacts. Advertising • The free version shows banner ads through Google AdMob. • The App does not present the iOS App Tracking Transparency prompt, so ads are not personalised using your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). • AdMob still receives the information any ad request carries, such as IP address, device type, and coarse location inferred from the IP address, under Google's privacy policy. • Buying the ad-free in-app purchase stops banners from being requested at all. Purchases • The ad-free upgrade is a one-time purchase processed by Apple. We receive your purchase status only; we never see your payment details. The App simply records a flag on your device that ads are off. Apple Watch • The Apple Watch companion fetches the same public air quality readings from our API. It does not collect anything additional. Who your data is shared with • Google (Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Cloud Messaging, AdMob, Google Maps), Bugsnag, Sentry and Apple, each acting under its own privacy policy for the purposes described above. • We do not sell your data and we do not share it with data brokers. Data retention • On-device settings and favourites stay on your device until you delete the App. • Alert settings on our server are kept so they can be restored if you reinstall the App. To have them removed, contact us through "Feedback / Contact us". • Analytics and crash reports are kept by those providers under their own standard retention schedules; we do not keep a separate copy. • Our API serves public air quality data and does not store personal data. Ordinary server logs may record request metadata such as IP addresses for a short period for security and troubleshooting. Your choices • Turn location off in iOS Settings › Privacy › Location Services. • Turn notifications off in iOS Settings, or disable individual station alerts inside the App. • Buy the ad-free upgrade to remove ads and ad requests. • Delete the App to remove all data held on your device. Children • The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Changes • We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date above will change. Contact • Questions? Use "Feedback / Contact us" on the Help screen in the App to reach us. 繁體中文版本： 隱私權政策
