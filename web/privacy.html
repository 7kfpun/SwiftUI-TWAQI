Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026 Taiwan Air ("the App", App Store ID 1286516443, bundle com.kfpun.twaqi) shows air quality readings from government monitoring stations in Taiwan on iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and Apple Vision Pro. It is made by an independent developer. We keep data collection to a minimum. This policy explains what is collected, why, and who it is shared with. Summary • No accounts. You can use the App without telling us who you are. • Your list of favourites and most settings stay on your device. The exception is alerts: for a station with an alert on — including one switched on when you favourite it — the station and its alert settings are stored on our own server so the alert can be delivered (see Notifications). • Location is optional and is only used on your device. • We receive your name and email address only if you send us the Feedback / Contact us form or the Business partner form. Where the air quality data comes from • Readings are open data published by Taiwan's Ministry of Environment (環境部, formerly the Environmental Protection Administration). The App fetches them through our own API hosted on Amazon Web Services. • That data describes monitoring stations, not people. Requests to our API carry only a country code, a station ID and a row limit — no account, no device identifier and no coordinates. • The air quality zone forecast (空品區預報) is read from our content server at cms.wahthefox.com (Directus). Station webcam images are loaded from twaqi.app.wahthefox.com (Cloudflare). These requests carry no personal data. Information stored on your device • The App has no accounts. • Your settings stay on your device: the index you have selected (AQI, PM2.5, and so on), your favourite stations, the last map position, quiet-hours times, language, the nearest station last resolved, and whether Taiwan Air Pro is active. The Home Screen widget and Apple Watch app read the same settings from your device. Your list of favourites is not sent anywhere, but while a station has an alert on, its alert settings, your quiet-hours times, the App's language and whether Pro is active are also sent off the device (see Notifications). • Air quality readings, forecasts and webcam images are cached on the device for offline use and removed automatically after about a week. • This on-device data is removed when you delete the App. The one exception is the random device ID used for alerts (see Notifications): it is kept in the iOS Keychain with "this device only" accessibility (ThisDeviceOnly), so it may persist after you delete and reinstall the App on the same device. It is not carried to a new device by a backup or restore. Feedback / Contact us and Business partner forms • These forms are the only place the App asks for personal details, and sending them is entirely up to you. What you submit is sent over HTTPS to our Directus content management system at cms.wahthefox.com and stored there (in collections named twaqi_feedback and twaqi_cooperate). • Feedback / Contact us (Help screen) sends: your email address (required, so we can reply), your name (optional), your star rating, your suggestion, any other feedback or question (optional), and whether you are interested in a user interview. • Business partner (Help › "Interested to be our Business Partner?") sends: the type of cooperation, company name, your name, email address, job title (optional), phone number (optional), website (optional) and your message. • To help us look into problems, both forms also send technical details: app version and build, the App's bundle ID, device model and type (for example iPhone, iPad or Apple Vision Pro), iOS version, language and region settings including your top three preferred languages, time zone and platform. The Feedback / Contact us form also includes your device's identifier for vendor (shared by our apps on your device; resets only when all our apps are removed) so repeat reports from the same device can be grouped; the Business partner form does not send it. • We use this information only to read and reply to your message, follow up on a partnership enquiry, invite you to a user interview if you asked to be invited, and improve the App. We do not add you to marketing lists and we do not sell it. Location • Location access is optional and is requested only while you are using the App ("Use location to select your nearest air quality monitoring station"). • Your coordinates are used on the device to centre the map and to pick the closest monitoring station. They are not sent to our API or to our content server, and we do not store them. • The map itself is drawn by the Google Maps SDK. Google may process location and device data for map display under Google's own privacy policy. • If you decline location access the App still works; it falls back to a default station and to whichever station you choose on the map. Notifications • Push notifications are delivered through Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service, which receive your device's push tokens and standard device information. • The App is also registered with OneSignal, a push notification service, so that we can use it to send alerts in future. OneSignal receives your device's push token, standard device information and, as tags, the same alert settings: the station, the threshold for each alert that is on, and your quiet-hours times. We do not currently send notifications through OneSignal. • If you turn on alerts for a station, your alert settings are stored on our Directus server (cms.wahthefox.com) so the alert can be sent. They contain a random device ID generated by the App and stored in the iOS Keychain (so it may persist after a reinstall on the same device, and lets your alerts come back after one), a one-way hash of a random secret kept next to that ID (the secret proves that changes come from your device; our server stores only its hash), the push tokens, app version, the App's language (so alerts arrive in it), whether Taiwan Air Pro is active, whether notifications are allowed for the App in iOS Settings (so we stop sending when they are off), time zone, your quiet-hours setting, and for each station with alerts: the station, the index, the threshold and whether the alert is on. They contain no name, email or location. • Turning an alert off (or cancelling all alerts) marks the matching rows on our server as inactive (archived); they are not deleted. Deleting the App stops delivery but does not remove them from our server. To have them deleted, send a request through "Feedback / Contact us". • Each alert we send is also logged on the same server so we can check delivery: the station, the AQI, why it was sent, whether it was delivered, and only the last six characters of the push token (never the whole token). Log rows are archived, not deleted, and are covered by the same deletion request. • Favouriting a station (the heart on its station screen) also switches on an alert for it: a notification when AQI rises above 120 (unhealthy for sensitive groups), which you can change or turn off on the station screen. This happens only if notifications are allowed for the App and fewer than three stations already have alerts on (the App's limit); otherwise the station is only saved as a favourite, no alert is switched on and nothing about it is sent. When the alert is switched on, the station and its threshold are stored on our server exactly as described above. • A station with an alert switched on is kept in your favourites so you can find it again. Removing a station from your favourites turns its alerts off, as above. Analytics and crash reporting • Google Firebase Analytics records anonymous usage events — for example which air index you select, opening a station, or starting, completing or restoring a purchase — together with standard device and app information. • Google Firebase Crashlytics, Bugsnag and Sentry receive crash and error reports: stack traces, device model, OS version and app version. These help us fix bugs. • None of this is used to identify you personally, and we do not link it to the details you send in the forms. Advertising • The free version shows banner ads through Google AdMob. • The App does not present the iOS App Tracking Transparency prompt, so ads are not personalised using your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). • AdMob still receives the information any ad request carries, such as IP address, device type, and coarse location inferred from the IP address, under Google's privacy policy. • While Taiwan Air Pro is active (a current subscription or the lifetime purchase), no banners are requested at all. Purchases • Taiwan Air Pro is optional and is sold through Apple as an auto-renewing subscription (1 month, 6 months or 12 months) or as a lifetime purchase. Anyone who bought the earlier one-time ad-free upgrade keeps Pro for life. • Apple processes every payment, renewal, cancellation and refund. We never see your payment details, your Apple ID or your name; the App only learns from Apple on your device whether Pro is active. • The App keeps that yes/no status on your device, shared with its Home Screen widgets and Apple Watch app (and, while a station has an alert on, with the alert settings on our server; see Notifications), and re-checks it with Apple so that a subscription that ends or is refunded switches Pro off. Restoring purchases asks Apple for the same status. • Subscriptions are managed and cancelled in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions; see our Terms of Service for renewal details. Apple Watch, widgets and Apple Vision Pro • The Apple Watch app, complications, Home Screen widgets and the Apple Vision Pro app fetch the same public air quality readings. They do not collect anything additional. Who your data is shared with • Service providers acting for the purposes described above, each under its own privacy policy: Google (Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Cloud Messaging, AdMob, Google Maps), OneSignal, Bugsnag, Sentry, Amazon Web Services, Cloudflare and Apple. • Form submissions and alert settings are stored on our own Directus server and are only accessible to us. • We do not sell your data and we do not share it with data brokers. Data retention • On-device settings and your list of favourites stay on your device until you delete the App. Alert settings sent to our server are kept as described below. • Feedback and Business partner submissions are kept for as long as we need them to reply, follow up and improve the App. When we no longer need one we archive it; you can ask us to delete yours at any time (see Your choices). • Alert settings on our server are marked inactive (archived) when you turn alerts off, and are kept until you ask us to delete them; deleting the App does not remove them. • Analytics and crash reports are kept by those providers under their own standard retention schedules; we do not keep a separate copy. • Our API and image server serve public data and do not store personal data. Ordinary server logs may record request metadata such as IP addresses for a short period for security and troubleshooting. Your choices • Turn location off in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services. • Turn notifications off in iOS Settings, or disable individual station alerts (or cancel all alerts) inside the App. • Get Taiwan Air Pro (subscription or lifetime) to remove ads and ad requests. • Don't send the forms if you don't want us to have your name and email — the rest of the App works without them. • To see, correct or delete what you sent us through a form, send a request through "Feedback / Contact us" from the email address you used, and we will act on it. • Delete the App to remove the data held on your device, apart from the Keychain device ID described above, which may persist after a reinstall on the same device. • To have your alert settings deleted from our server, send a request through "Feedback / Contact us". Children • The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Changes • We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date above will change.
